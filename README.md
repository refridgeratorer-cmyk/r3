# Part 3 — The Reckoning

Stable combat revision focused on the final boss identity and boss balance.

## Final boss: YOU
- Mirrors the player's owned weapons and weapon levels.
- Cycles between the player's weapons during the fight.
- Uses mirrored attack patterns for Memory Blade, Burden Hammer, Regret Needles, Echo, and Forgiven.
- Mirrors selected run upgrades such as Acceptance, Patience, Courage, Weightless, Memory, and Forgiveness.
- The HUD labels which weapon YOU are currently mirroring.

## Boss balance
- The Past HP: 520
- The Weight HP: 700
- The Unknown HP: 820
- The Absence HP: 460 (reduced from the previous version)
- YOU HP: 760

## Notes
- Boss-owned projectiles cannot damage their owner.
- Boss projectiles spawn outside the boss body.
- The final boss's attacks inherit the player's current run build.
